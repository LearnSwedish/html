German verb in focus

LAUFENin Swedish

The German verb LAUFEN covers several everyday meanings that Swedish normally expresses with different verbs. The correct Swedish verb depends on what is moving, functioning, being shown or remaining valid.

With LAUFEN, identify the event first: a person may run, water may flow, a motor may work, a film may be showing, an activity may be in progress, or an agreement may remain valid.

Why LAUFEN is difficult in Swedish

German laufen is much broader than Swedish springa. The same German verb is used for people, liquids, machines, films, ongoing activities and periods of validity.

Swedish normally separates these meanings and chooses a different verb for each one.

What does LAUFEN mean here?

Is a person moving at a run? → springa
Is a liquid flowing? → rinna
Is a machine or system working? → fungera
Is a film or programme being shown? → visas
Is an activity still in progress? → pågå
Is something valid for a particular period? → gälla

Six Swedish ways to translate LAUFEN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the German verb. The examples are in German, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

springa
run · move quickly on foot

Use springa when laufen describes a person moving at a run.

Der Junge läuft.
Pojken springer.
Ich laufe jeden Morgen im Park.
Jag springer varje morgon i parken.
Sie läuft fünf Kilometer.
Hon springer fem kilometer.
In this sense, laufen describes running. Swedish uses springa rather than the broader German verb.
rinna
flow · run as a liquid

Use rinna when laufen describes a liquid moving or flowing.

Das Wasser läuft.
Vattnet rinner.
Das Wasser läuft aus dem Hahn.
Vattnet rinner ur kranen.
Der Schweiß läuft ihm über das Gesicht.
Svetten rinner över hans ansikte.
When a liquid moves continuously, Swedish normally uses rinna. The German verb remains laufen.
fungera
work · operate

Use fungera when laufen means that a machine, motor or system is operating properly.

Der Motor läuft.
Motorn fungerar.
Die Maschine läuft wieder.
Maskinen fungerar igen.
Das Programm läuft ohne Probleme.
Programmet fungerar utan problem.
German often uses laufen for something that is operating. Swedish normally states this explicitly with fungera.
visas
be shown · be on

Use visas when laufen means that a film, programme or performance is currently being shown.

Der Film läuft im Kino.
Filmen visas på bio.
Was läuft heute Abend im Fernsehen?
Vad visas på tv i kväll?
Der Film läuft noch bis Donnerstag.
Filmen visas fortfarande till torsdag.
For films and programmes, German laufen refers to being on or being shown. Swedish normally uses visas.
pågå
be in progress · continue

Use pågå when laufen describes an activity, process or event that is continuing.

Die Verhandlungen laufen noch.
Förhandlingarna pågår fortfarande.
Das Projekt läuft seit Januar.
Projektet pågår sedan januari.
Der Wettbewerb läuft noch eine Woche.
Tävlingen pågår i en vecka till.
When the focus is duration or continuation, Swedish normally uses pågå. Nothing is literally running.
gälla
be valid · remain in force

Use gälla when laufen refers to a contract, guarantee, offer or similar arrangement being valid for a stated period.

Der Vertrag läuft bis Dezember.
Avtalet gäller till december.
Die Garantie läuft noch zwei Jahre.
Garantin gäller i två år till.
Das Angebot läuft nur bis Freitag.
Erbjudandet gäller bara till fredag.
Here laufen describes the period during which something remains in force. Swedish normally uses gälla.
