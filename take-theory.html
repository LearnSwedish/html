TAKEin Swedish

The English verb TAKE appears in many everyday combinations involving transport, photographs, medicine, events, participation and sitting down. Swedish divides these uses between several distinct verbs and verb phrases.

With TAKE, look at the noun that follows: it usually identifies the Swedish verb you need.

Why TAKE is difficult in Swedish

English uses take in numerous established combinations: take the bus, take a photo, take medicine, take place, take part, take a seat.

These combinations cannot normally be translated by repeating Swedish ta. Each expression has its own natural Swedish equivalent.

What does TAKE mean here?

Is somebody travelling by bus or train? → åka
Is somebody taking a photograph? → fotografera
Is somebody consuming medicine? → ta
Is the subject an event occurring? → äga rum
Is somebody participating? → delta
Is somebody being invited to sit? → sätta sig

Six Swedish ways to translate TAKE

Each choice below expresses a different type of event. The English examples use ordinary British English, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

åka
travel · take transport

Use åka when take means travelling by bus, train, underground or another form of transport.

I take the bus to work.
Jag åker buss till jobbet.
We took the train to Stockholm.
Vi åkte tåg till Stockholm.
Do you usually take the underground?
Brukar du åka tunnelbana?
Swedish can also say ta bussen or ta tåget. Åka makes the travel meaning explicit.
fotografera
photograph · take a photo

Use fotografera when take refers to producing a photograph.

She takes a lot of photos.
Hon fotograferar mycket.
May I take a photo of you?
Får jag fotografera dig?
He took photos of the building from several angles.
Han fotograferade byggnaden ur flera vinklar.
The equally natural phrase ta ett foto is common, but fotografera provides the distinct Swedish verb represented here.
ta
take · consume medicine

Use ta when the object is medicine, tablets or another prescribed dose.

I take this medicine twice a day.
Jag tar den här medicinen två gånger om dagen.
Have you taken your tablets?
Har du tagit dina tabletter?
Take one tablet with food.
Ta en tablett i samband med måltid.
This is one of the contexts in which English take and Swedish ta correspond directly.
äga rum
happen · take place

Use äga rum when take place means that an event happens at a particular time or place.

The meeting takes place on Monday.
Mötet äger rum på måndag.
The ceremony took place outdoors.
Ceremonin ägde rum utomhus.
Where will the conference take place?
Var ska konferensen äga rum?
Do not translate the individual words take and place. The whole expression corresponds to Swedish äga rum.
delta
participate · take part

Use delta when take part means participating in an activity, event or discussion.

She took part in the meeting.
Hon deltog i mötet.
Would you like to take part?
Vill du delta?
Over a hundred people took part in the race.
Över hundra personer deltog i loppet.
Swedish delta is followed by i when the activity is stated: delta i mötet.
sätta sig
sit down · take a seat

Use sätta sig when take a seat means sitting down.

Please take a seat.
Var så god och sätt dig.
He took a seat by the window.
Han satte sig vid fönstret.
We took our seats at the back.
Vi satte oss längst bak.
Swedish normally describes the movement into a seated position with sätta sig.

How TAKE divides into six meanings

Use the meaning of the complete expression — not the English verb by itself — to choose the Swedish equivalent.

Important: English also has more specific verbs and expressions. The difficulty is that everyday English allows take in all six situations, whereas Swedish normally makes the semantic category more explicit.
