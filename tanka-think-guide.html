Swedish for English speakers

Think twice about think

English has one very flexible verb: think. Swedish divides much of that work between tänka, tycka, and tro. At the same time, Swedish tänka itself can mean more than English think.

One English word → three Swedish verbs

Think may describe a mental process, a personal opinion, or an uncertain belief. Swedish usually chooses a different verb for each.

↔

One Swedish verb → several English expressions

Tänka can mean to think, but it can also express an intention or ask someone to explain their reasoning.

Direction 1

When English says “think”

Do not translate the word first. Ask what kind of “thinking” the sentence expresses.

tänka

mental activity

Låt mig tänka.

Let me think.

tycka

personal opinion

Jag tycker hon är fantastisk.

I think she is fantastic.

tro

belief or probability

Jag tror han är hemma.

I think he is at home.

Direction 2

When Swedish says “tänka”

Tänka is not limited to silent thought. The surrounding grammar helps reveal its role.

Cognitive process
Jag behöver tänka.
I need to think.
Intention or plan
Jag tänker stanna hemma.
I intend / plan to stay at home.
Reasoning or intended meaning
Hur tänker du då?
What do you mean? / What is your reasoning?

The useful shortcut

Think with your mind → tänka. Give a subjective evaluation → tycka. Say what you believe is probably true → tro.

Am I processing an idea?Choose tänka.
Am I giving my opinion?Choose tycka.
Am I uncertain about a fact?Choose tro.
