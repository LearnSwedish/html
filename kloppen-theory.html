Dutch verb in focus

KLOPPENin Swedish

The Dutch verb KLOPPEN has several distinct meanings that Swedish normally expresses with different verbs. It can mean knocking, beating, being correct, whisking, defeating an opponent, or beating dust from a rug.

With KLOPPEN, identify the event first: somebody may knock, a heart may beat, information may be correct, cream may be whisked, an opponent may be defeated, or a rug may be beaten.

Why KLOPPEN is difficult in Swedish

Dutch kloppen is highly polysemous. Swedish normally separates these meanings more clearly and chooses a different verb according to the situation.

Swedish normally separates these meanings and chooses a different verb for each one.

What does KLOPPEN mean here?

Is somebody knocking? → knacka
Is a heart beating? → slå
Is something correct? → stämma
Is food being whisked? → vispa
Is an opponent being defeated? → besegra
Is a rug being beaten clean? → piska

Six Swedish ways to translate KLOPPEN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the Dutch verb. The examples are in Dutch, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

knacka
knock

Use knacka when kloppen means making a knocking sound to attract attention.

Er klopt iemand.
Någon knackar.
De buurman klopt.
Grannen knackar.
Iemand klopt drie keer.
Någon knackar tre gånger.
When kloppen means knocking to attract attention, Swedish normally uses knacka.
slå
beat · pulsate

Use slå when kloppen describes the beating of the heart.

Zijn hart klopt snel.
Hans hjärta slår snabbt.
Mijn hart klopt harder.
Mitt hjärta slår snabbare.
Haar hart klopte rustig.
Hennes hjärta slog lugnt.
For a heartbeat, Swedish uses slå. The Dutch verb is still kloppen.
stämma
be correct · be right

Use stämma when kloppen means that information, figures or a statement are correct.

Dat klopt.
Det stämmer.
Het verhaal klopt niet.
Berättelsen stämmer inte.
De cijfers kloppen.
Siffrorna stämmer.
In this abstract sense, kloppen means being correct or consistent. Swedish normally uses stämma.
vispa
whisk · beat

Use vispa when kloppen means beating or whisking food.

Zij klopt de slagroom.
Hon vispar grädden.
Hij klopt de eieren.
Han vispar äggen.
Klop de saus goed.
Vispa såsen ordentligt.
In cooking, Dutch kloppen can mean beating or whisking ingredients. Swedish uses vispa.
besegra
defeat · beat an opponent

Use besegra when kloppen means defeating an opponent in sport or competition.

Ajax klopt PSV.
Ajax besegrar PSV.
De kampioen klopte zijn uitdager.
Mästaren besegrade sin utmanare.
Ons team klopte de favoriet.
Vårt lag besegrade favoriten.
When kloppen means winning against an opponent, Swedish normally uses besegra.
piska
beat · beat a rug

Use piska when kloppen means beating a rug or carpet to remove dust.

Hij klopt de mat.
Han piskar mattan.
Ze klopt het tapijt buiten.
Hon piskar mattan utomhus.
De schoonmaker klopt het vloerkleed.
Städaren piskar mattan.
When a rug is beaten to remove dust, Swedish normally uses piska rather than slå.
