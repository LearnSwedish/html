Dutch verb in focus

LOPENin Swedish

The Dutch verb LOPEN covers several everyday meanings that Swedish normally expresses with different verbs. The correct Swedish verb depends on whether somebody is walking, a liquid is flowing, a machine is working, a film is being shown, an activity is in progress or an agreement remains valid.

With LOPEN, identify the event first: a person may walk, water may flow, a motor may work, a film may be showing, an activity may be in progress, or an agreement may remain valid.

Why LOPEN is difficult in Swedish

Dutch lopen is much broader than Swedish gå. The same Dutch verb is used for people moving on foot, liquids, machines, films, ongoing activities and periods of validity.

Swedish normally separates these meanings and chooses a different verb for each one.

gårinnafungeravisaspågågälla

What does LOPEN mean here?

Is a person moving on foot? → gå
Is a liquid flowing? → rinna
Is a machine or system working? → fungera
Is a film or programme being shown? → visas
Is an activity still in progress? → pågå
Is something valid for a particular period? → gälla

Six Swedish ways to translate LOPEN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the Dutch verb. The examples are in Dutch, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

gå
walk · move on foot

Use gå when lopen describes a person moving on foot.

Ik loop elke ochtend naar mijn werk.
Jag går till jobbet varje morgon.
De kinderen lopen naar school.
Barnen går till skolan.
Hij loopt langzaam door het park.
Han går långsamt genom parken.
In this sense, lopen describes ordinary movement on foot. Swedish normally uses gå.
rinna
flow · run as a liquid

Use rinna when lopen describes a liquid moving or flowing.

Het water loopt uit de kraan.
Vattnet rinner ur kranen.
Het zweet loopt over zijn gezicht.
Svetten rinner över hans ansikte.
De verf loopt langs de muur.
Färgen rinner längs väggen.
When a liquid moves continuously, Swedish normally uses rinna.
fungera
work · operate

Use fungera when lopen means that a machine or system is operating properly.

De motor loopt weer.
Motorn fungerar igen.
De machine loopt zonder problemen.
Maskinen fungerar utan problem.
Het systeem loopt goed.
Systemet fungerar bra.
Dutch often uses lopen for something that is operating. Swedish normally uses fungera.
visas
be shown · be on

Use visas when lopen means that a film or programme is currently being shown.

De film loopt in drie bioscopen.
Filmen visas på tre biografer.
Wat loopt er vanavond op televisie?
Vad visas på tv i kväll?
De documentaire loopt nog tot vrijdag.
Dokumentären visas fortfarande till fredag.
For films and programmes, lopen can refer to being on or being shown. Swedish normally uses visas.
pågå
be in progress · continue

Use pågå when lopen describes an activity, process or event that is continuing.

De vergadering loopt nog.
Mötet pågår fortfarande.
Het onderzoek loopt sinds januari.
Undersökningen pågår sedan januari.
De actie loopt nog een week.
Kampanjen pågår i en vecka till.
When the focus is duration or continuation, Swedish normally uses pågå.
gälla
be valid · remain in force

Use gälla when lopen refers to a contract, insurance policy, subscription or similar arrangement being valid for a stated period.

Het contract loopt tot december.
Avtalet gäller till december.
De verzekering loopt nog een jaar.
Försäkringen gäller i ett år till.
Het abonnement loopt tot eind juni.
Abonnemanget gäller till slutet av juni.
Here lopen describes the period during which something remains in force. Swedish normally uses gälla.
