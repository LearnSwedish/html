GET in Swedish

The English verb GET is unusually broad. In ordinary speech it can cover receiving something, changing state, arriving somewhere, understanding something, earning money, or obtaining something deliberately. Swedish normally makes those distinctions explicit by choosing a different verb.

GET does not have one fixed Swedish equivalent. First identify what GET means in the sentence.

Why GET is difficult in Swedish

In English, get can replace several more specific verbs in informal or neutral everyday language: receive, become, arrive, understand, earn, obtain.

Swedish does not usually keep one equally broad verb for all these meanings. You therefore have to identify the semantic role of get before choosing the Swedish verb.

Six Swedish ways to translate GET

Each verb below expresses a different type of event. The examples deliberately use natural British English with get, followed by the Swedish sentence that an English speaker would actually need.

få
receive · be given · get

Use få when something comes to you: a message, a present, an answer, permission, a chance. The focus is on receiving, not on actively acquiring.

I get a lot of emails at work.
Jag får många mejl på jobbet.
She gets a present every year.
Hon får en present varje år.
Do we get another chance?
Får vi en chans till?
Contrast with skaffa: få is typically receptive; skaffa is typically deliberate.
bli
become · get + adjective/state

Use bli when somebody or something changes from one state to another. English very often uses get + adjective here.

I get tired after lunch.
Jag blir trött efter lunch.
It gets dark early in winter.
Det blir mörkt tidigt på vintern.
He gets angry very easily.
Han blir arg väldigt lätt.
English getting tired may sometimes be translated as börjar bli trött when the change is explicitly gradual. The core Swedish verb is still bli.
komma
come · arrive · reach

Use komma when get refers to arriving at a place or reaching a destination. The important idea is movement towards an endpoint.

I get home at six.
Jag kommer hem klockan sex.
What time do you get to work?
Vilken tid kommer du till jobbet?
We get there just before nine.
Vi kommer dit strax före nio.
English get to can also mean “have the opportunity to”, as in I get to meet her. That is a different meaning and does not use komma.
förstå
understand · grasp · get

Use förstå when get means mentally grasping a meaning, explanation, joke, point or situation.

I get the joke now.
Jag förstår skämtet nu.
Do you get what I mean?
Förstår du vad jag menar?
I still don't get the question.
Jag förstår fortfarande inte frågan.
In informal Swedish, fatta is also common: Jag fattar = “I get it.” Förstå is the neutral standard choice.
tjäna
earn · make (money)

Use tjäna when get describes money received as pay or income. Unlike få, the money is understood as earned through work or activity.

I get £25 an hour.
Jag tjänar 25 pund i timmen.
She gets £3,000 a month.
Hon tjänar 3 000 pund i månaden.
How much do you get a year?
Hur mycket tjänar du om året?
If the point is simply that money is received — for example a grant or gift — Swedish may use få instead.
skaffa
obtain · acquire · get hold of

Use skaffa when someone actively makes sure they obtain something: a new phone, a job, a ticket, a dog, new equipment.

I get a new phone every three years.
Jag skaffar en ny telefon vart tredje år.
We need to get a bigger table.
Vi måste skaffa ett större bord.
He wants to get a dog.
Han vill skaffa hund.
Skaffa is broader than “buy”. You can skaffa something by buying it, borrowing it, arranging it, finding it or otherwise obtaining it.

How GET divides into six meanings

These are the distinctions that matter most when choosing the Swedish verb.

Swedish verb Core meaning What the subject is doing Typical English GET pattern Diagnostic question
få receive receiving something that comes to them get an email / get a present Does something come to the person?
bli become changing state get tired / get angry / get dark Is there a change from one state to another?
komma come / arrive reaching a place get home / get there Is the subject arriving somewhere?
förstå understand grasping meaning get the joke / get the point Is the issue comprehension?
tjäna earn receiving money as income get £25 an hour Is the money earned?
skaffa obtain / acquire actively obtaining something get a phone / get a dog Is the subject deliberately making sure they obtain it?
Important: English has the more specific verbs too: receive, become, arrive, understand, earn, obtain. The challenge is that everyday English often allows get in all six situations, whereas Swedish normally requires the speaker to choose the semantic category more explicitly.
