Dutch verb in focus

DRAAIENin Swedish

The Dutch verb DRAAIEN has several distinct meanings that Swedish normally expresses with different verbs. It can describe rotation, a change of direction, a machine operating, a film being shown, work on a lathe, or rolling a cigarette.

With DRAAIEN, identify what kind of turning or operation is involved before choosing the Swedish verb.

Why DRAAIEN is difficult in Swedish

Dutch draaien covers several meanings that Swedish keeps separate. Swedish distinguishes between something spinning, something turning direction, a machine running, a film being shown, material being turned on a lathe, and a cigarette being rolled.

Swedish normally separates these meanings and chooses a different verb for each one.

What does DRAAIEN mean here?

Is something rotating? → snurra
Is something changing direction? → vända
Is a machine running? → gå
Is a film being shown? → visas
Is material being worked on a lathe? → svarva
Is a cigarette being made by rolling? → rulla

Six Swedish ways to translate DRAAIEN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the Dutch verb. The examples are in Dutch, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

snurra
spin · rotate

Use snurra when draaien describes something rotating around an axis.

Het wiel draait.
Hjulet snurrar.
De propeller draait snel.
Propellern snurrar snabbt.
De plaat draait op de speler.
Skivan snurrar på spelaren.
In this sense, draaien refers to continuous rotation. Swedish normally uses snurra.
vända
turn · change direction

Use vända when draaien means changing direction or orientation.

De wind draait naar het westen.
Vinden vänder mot väster.
Het tij draait.
Tidvattnet vänder.
De stemming draaide plotseling.
Stämningen vände plötsligt.
Here draaien describes a change in direction or development. Swedish normally uses vända.
gå
run · be operating

Use gå when draaien means that a motor or machine is running.

De motor draait.
Motorn går.
De machine draait dag en nacht.
Maskinen går dag och natt.
De motor draait nog.
Motorn går fortfarande.
When a machine is operating, Swedish often uses the broad verb gå.
visas
be shown · be on

Use visas when draaien means that a film is being shown.

De film draait in de bioscoop.
Filmen visas på bio.
Deze documentaire draait deze week.
Den här dokumentären visas den här veckan.
Wat draait er vanavond?
Vad visas i kväll?
For films, Dutch draaien can mean being on or being shown. Swedish normally uses visas.
svarva
turn on a lathe

Use svarva when draaien means shaping wood or metal on a lathe.

Hij draait hout.
Han svarvar trä.
Zij draait een houten kom.
Hon svarvar en träskål.
De vakman draait metalen onderdelen.
Hantverkaren svarvar metalldelar.
In craft and manufacturing contexts, draaien can refer to turning material on a lathe. Swedish uses svarva.
rulla
roll · make by rolling

Use rulla when draaien means rolling a cigarette.

Hij draait een sigaret.
Han rullar en cigarett.
Ze draait zelf haar sigaretten.
Hon rullar sina cigaretter själv.
Hij draait een dunne sigaret.
Han rullar en tunn cigarett.
When draaien means making a cigarette by rolling it, Swedish uses rulla.
