English verb in focus

MAKEin Swedish

The English verb MAKE covers general creation, cooking, deciding, causing a reaction, reaching transport and preparing a bed. Swedish normally identifies the particular action and chooses a more specific verb.

MAKE does not have one fixed Swedish equivalent. Ask what is actually being produced, caused or achieved.

Why MAKE is difficult in Swedish

English uses make for many kinds of general creation and achievement: make dinner, make a choice, make a decision, make someone laugh.

Swedish normally names the activity more precisely. Before translating make, decide whether somebody is cooking, general creating/doing, deciding, causing, catching transport or preparing a bed.

What does MAKE mean here?

Is somebody preparing a meal? → laga
Is something being made or produced? → göra
Is somebody deciding something? → fatta
Does one thing cause somebody to act or react? → få
Is somebody reaching transport in time? → hinna med
Is somebody preparing a bed? → bädda

Six Swedish ways to translate MAKE

Each choice below expresses a different type of event. The English examples use ordinary British English, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

laga
cook · prepare food

Use laga when make means preparing cooked food or a meal.

I make dinner every evening.
Jag lagar middag varje kväll.
She is making soup.
Hon lagar soppa.
Who made dinner?
Vem lagade middagen?
Use baka, not laga, when the activity is specifically baking bread, cakes or biscuits.
göra
make · produce

Use göra when make refers to general creation, execution or bringing something about.

She makes a good point.
Hon gör en bra poäng.
We can make an exception this time.
Vi kan göra ett undantag den här gången.
He made a big effort.
Han gjorde en stor ansträngning.
Depending on the exact context, other stylistic alternatives like skapa (create), tillverka (manufacture) or producera (produce) can also correspond to English make.
fatta
make · reach a decision

Use fatta with beslut when a person or organisation formally or deliberately makes a decision.

We need to make a decision today.
Vi måste fatta ett beslut i dag.
She made the right decision.
Hon fattade rätt beslut.
The board has not made a final decision.
Styrelsen har inte fattat något slutgiltigt beslut.
In everyday Swedish, bestämma sig is also common when the emphasis is on a person making up their mind.
få
cause · make someone do

Use få + object + att + infinitive when make means causing somebody to do something.

You always make me laugh.
Du får mig alltid att skratta.
The question made me think.
Frågan fick mig att tänka.
The noise made the dog bark.
Ljudet fick hunden att skälla.
English make someone do normally becomes få någon att göra. Swedish requires att before the infinitive.
hinna med
catch · reach in time

Use hinna med when make means arriving in time for a train, bus, flight or other departure.

I think we can make the train.
Jag tror att vi hinner med tåget.
Did you make your flight?
Hann du med flyget?
Leave now if you want to make the bus.
Gå nu om du vill hinna med bussen.
The central idea in hinna med is having enough time before something departs or begins.
bädda
make the bed

Use bädda when make refers to arranging the sheets, duvet and pillows on a bed.

I make the bed every morning.
Jag bäddar sängen varje morgon.
Have you made your bed?
Har du bäddat sängen?
The children make their own beds.
Barnen bäddar sina egna sängar.
Swedish uses the specific verb bädda; a literal translation with göra is not idiomatic.

How MAKE divides into six meanings

Use the meaning of the complete expression — not the English verb by itself — to choose the Swedish equivalent.

Swedish choiceCore meaningTypical English MAKE patternDiagnostic question
laga preparing cooked food I make dinner every evening. Is somebody preparing a meal?
göra general execution, actions or creation She makes a good point. Is something being made or produced?
fatta making a decision We need to make a decision today. Is somebody deciding something?

bädda arranging bedding I make the bed every morning. Is somebody preparing a bed?
Important: English also has more specific verbs and expressions. The difficulty is that everyday English allows make in all six situations, whereas Swedish normally makes the semantic category more explicit.
