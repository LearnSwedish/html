BRANDENin Swedish

The Dutch verb BRANDEN covers several meanings that Swedish normally expresses with different verbs. It can describe fire, a burning sensation, a light being on, writing data to a disc, roasting coffee, or strong sunshine.

With BRANDEN, first decide whether the meaning concerns fire, pain, light, data, roasting or intense sunshine.

Why BRANDEN is difficult in Swedish

Dutch branden is broader than Swedish brinna or bränna. Swedish normally chooses a more specific verb according to what is burning or what effect is being described.

Swedish normally separates these meanings and chooses a different verb for each one.

What does BRANDEN mean here?

Is something itself on fire? → brinna
Is there a burning or stinging sensation? → svida
Is a lamp or light on? → lysa
Is data being written to an optical disc? → bränna
Is coffee being roasted? → rosta
Is the sun shining intensely? → gassa

Six Swedish ways to translate BRANDEN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the Dutch verb. The examples are in Dutch, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

brinna
burn · be on fire

Use brinna when branden describes something that is burning or on fire.

Het huis brandt.
Huset brinner.
Het vuur brandt nog.
Elden brinner fortfarande.
De kaars brandt.
Ljuset brinner.
When something itself is burning, Swedish uses the intransitive verb brinna.
svida
sting · burn painfully

Use svida when branden describes a burning or stinging physical sensation.

Mijn ogen branden.
Mina ögon svider.
De wond brandt.
Såret svider.
Mijn huid brandt na de zonnebrand.
Min hud svider efter solbrännan.
When branden refers to a painful burning sensation, Swedish normally uses svida.
lysa
shine · be lit

Use lysa when branden means that a lamp or light is on.

De lamp brandt.
Lampan lyser.
Het licht brandt nog.
Ljuset lyser fortfarande.
In de keuken brandt een klein lampje.
I köket lyser en liten lampa.
A Dutch lamp can literally branden. Swedish normally describes the same state with lysa.
bränna
burn · write data to a disc

Use bränna when branden means writing data to a CD, DVD or other optical disc.

Hij brandt een cd.
Han bränner en cd.
Ze brandt de bestanden op een schijf.
Hon bränner filerna på en skiva.
Ik brand een kopie voor je.
Jag bränner en kopia åt dig.
In this technological sense, Dutch and Swedish use closely related verbs: branden and bränna.
rosta
roast · roast coffee beans

Use rosta when branden means roasting coffee or coffee beans.

Ze branden koffie.
De rostar kaffe.
De fabriek brandt dagelijks koffiebonen.
Fabriken rostar kaffebönor varje dag.
Hij brandt de bonen langzaam.
Han rostar bönorna långsamt.
For coffee, Dutch can use branden where Swedish uses the more specific verb rosta.
gassa
blaze · shine intensely

Use gassa when branden describes strong, intense sunshine.

De zon brandt.
Solen gassar.
De zon brandt de hele middag.
Solen gassar hela eftermiddagen.
Buiten brandt de zon fel.
Utanför gassar solen starkt.
When the sun is intensely hot and bright, Swedish can use the vivid verb gassa.
