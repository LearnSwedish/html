German verb in focus

ZIEHENin Swedish

The German verb ZIEHEN has several common lexical meanings that Swedish normally divides between different verbs. It can describe pulling, moving house, drifting, penetrating, cultivating and attracting.

With ZIEHEN, ask what kind of movement or effect is involved: physical pulling, relocation, drifting through the air, penetration, cultivation or attraction.

Why ZIEHEN is difficult in Swedish

German ziehen is not limited to the physical meaning ‘pull’. Clouds can ziehen, moisture can ziehen into a wall, people can ziehen to another city, and a film can ziehen an audience.

Swedish normally lexicalises these meanings separately.

What does ZIEHEN mean here?

Is something being pulled by force? → dra
Is somebody changing place of residence? → flytta
Is something moving slowly through the air? → driva
Is something penetrating into or through something? → tränga
Are plants being cultivated? → odla
Is something attracting people? → locka

Six Swedish ways to translate ZIEHEN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the German verb. The examples are in German, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

dra
pull · move by force

Use dra when ziehen means pulling something towards or behind the agent.

Er zieht den Wagen.
Han drar vagnen.
Das Pferd zieht den Wagen.
Hästen drar vagnen.
Er zieht den Koffer hinter sich her.
Han drar resväskan efter sig.
This is the most direct physical sense of ziehen: force is applied so that an object moves. Swedish uses dra.
flytta
move · change residence

Use flytta when ziehen means moving to a new home, town or country.

Sie zieht nach Berlin.
Hon flyttar till Berlin.
Wir ziehen nächstes Jahr nach Hamburg.
Vi flyttar till Hamburg nästa år.
Er zieht aus beruflichen Gründen nach München.
Han flyttar till München av arbetsskäl.
In this lexical sense, ziehen means changing residence. Swedish uses flytta, not dra.
driva
drift · move through the air

Use driva when ziehen describes clouds, fog, smoke or similar masses moving gradually through the air.

Die Wolken ziehen nach Osten.
Molnen driver österut.
Nebel zieht über das Tal.
Dimman driver över dalen.
Rauch zieht über die Dächer.
Rök driver över taken.
The movement is gradual and carried along rather than actively propelled. Swedish commonly uses driva.
tränga
penetrate · pass into

Use tränga when ziehen describes moisture, smoke, cold or similar phenomena entering or passing into something.

Die Feuchtigkeit zieht in die Wand.
Fukten tränger in i väggen.
Der Rauch zieht ins Zimmer.
Röken tränger in i rummet.
Die Kälte zieht durch die Fenster.
Kylan tränger in genom fönstren.
Here ziehen describes movement into or through a material or space. Swedish normally makes the penetrating movement explicit with tränga.
odla
grow · cultivate

Use odla when ziehen means cultivating or raising plants.

Sie zieht Rosen im Garten.
Hon odlar rosor i trädgården.
Er zieht Tomaten im Gewächshaus.
Han odlar tomater i växthuset.
Wir ziehen Kräuter auf dem Balkon.
Vi odlar örter på balkongen.
German can use ziehen for growing plants. Swedish normally uses the dedicated cultivation verb odla.
locka
attract · draw an audience

Use locka when ziehen means attracting people, visitors or an audience.

Der Film zieht viele Zuschauer.
Filmen lockar många åskådare.
Das Museum zieht jedes Jahr viele Besucher.
Museet lockar många besökare varje år.
Der Markt zieht Touristen aus der ganzen Region.
Marknaden lockar turister från hela regionen.
In this sense, ziehen expresses attraction rather than physical pulling. Swedish normally uses locka.
