German verb in focus

FÜHRENin Swedish

The German verb FÜHREN has several distinct meanings that Swedish normally expresses with different verbs. The correct Swedish choice depends on whether somebody is being guided, a business is being run, a team is leading, an instrument is being handled, a train carries something, or a shop stocks a product.

With FÜHREN, identify the lexical meaning first. The same German verb may refer to guiding, managing, leading, handling, carrying or stocking.

Why FÜHREN is difficult in Swedish

German führen is broader than any single Swedish verb. It can refer to showing people around, running a business, being ahead in a match, handling an instrument, carrying a particular carriage, or stocking a certain brand.

Swedish normally separates these meanings and chooses a different verb for each one.

What does FÜHREN mean here?

Is somebody showing people around? → guida
Is somebody running a business or organisation? → driva
Is somebody or something ahead in a competition? → leda
Is a tool or instrument being controlled skilfully? → hantera
Does a train, vehicle or list include something? → ha
Does a shop stock a particular product or brand? → sälja

Six Swedish ways to translate FÜHREN

Each Swedish verb below corresponds to a distinct lexical meaning of the German verb. The examples are in German, followed by the natural Swedish equivalent.

guida
guide · show around

Use guida when führen means showing visitors or guests around.

Der Museumsführer führt die Besucher.
Museiguiden guidar besökarna.
Sie führte die Gäste durch das Schloss.
Hon guidade gästerna genom slottet.
Er führt die Touristen durch die Altstadt.
Han guidar turisterna genom gamla stan.
Here führen means accompanying people and showing them the way or explaining what they see. Swedish normally uses guida.
driva
run · manage a business

Use driva when führen means running or managing a business or similar enterprise.

Sie führt ein kleines Hotel.
Hon driver ett litet hotell.
Er führt die Buchhandlung seit zehn Jahren.
Han driver bokhandeln sedan tio år.
Die Familie führt ein Restaurant am Marktplatz.
Familjen driver en restaurang vid torget.
In this meaning, führen refers to being in charge of a business or institution. Swedish normally uses driva.
leda
lead · be ahead

Use leda when führen means being ahead in a match, race or competition.

Unsere Mannschaft führt 2:0.
Vårt lag leder med 2–0.
Nach der ersten Runde führt sie klar.
Efter första omgången leder hon klart.
Wer führt im Rennen?
Vem leder i loppet?
When the meaning is being in first place, Swedish normally uses leda. Nothing is being guided here.
hantera
handle · control a tool or instrument

Use hantera when führen means handling or controlling an instrument, tool or implement.

Er führt das Skalpell sehr sicher.
Han hanterar skalpellen mycket säkert.
Sie führte den Pinsel mit ruhiger Hand.
Hon hanterade penseln med stadig hand.
Der Chirurg führte das Instrument vorsichtig.
Kirurgen hanterade instrumentet försiktigt.
This meaning refers to controlling an instrument precisely. Swedish normally makes this explicit with hantera.
ha
have · include or carry

Use ha when führen means including, carrying or containing something.

Der Zug führt einen Speisewagen.
Tåget har en restaurangvagn.
Die Liste führt alle Namen.
Listan har alla namn.
Der Katalog führt auch ältere Modelle.
Katalogen har också äldre modeller.
Here führen means that something includes or carries something else. Swedish often uses the simpler verb ha.
sälja
sell · stock a product

Use sälja when führen means that a shop or business stocks a particular product or brand.

Dieses Geschäft führt die Marke nicht.
Den här butiken säljer inte det märket.
Die Apotheke führt das Mittel nicht mehr.
Apoteket säljer inte längre det läkemedlet.
Wir führen nur regionale Produkte.
Vi säljer bara regionala produkter.
In retail contexts, führen often means keeping something in stock. Swedish normally expresses this with sälja.
